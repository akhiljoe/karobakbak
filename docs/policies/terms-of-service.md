## 1. What this is

karobakbak pairs you with a randomly chosen stranger for a text conversation.
When nobody is available, your partner may instead be one of our automated
accounts (section 3a). There is no account. You do not choose who you are
matched with, and neither does anyone else.

By using it, you agree to these terms. If you do not, do not use it.

## 2. You must be 18 or over

You must be **18 or over** to use this service. By using it you confirm that you
are.

We ask your age and we do not verify it. That means you should not assume anyone
you are matched with is an adult, whatever they tell you — see section 8.

If we have reason to believe a user is under 18, we will end their access.

## 3. What we keep, briefly

Your conversations are stored for 30 days, or 90 days if somebody reports them.
If a conversation is reported, we can read it — "we" being one person, not a
department. This is not a service where messages disappear when you close the
tab, and we would rather tell you that plainly than have you assume otherwise.

The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

## 3a. Some of the people here are not people

When nobody is available to match you with, we may match you with an automated
account that we run. It will behave like somebody chatting, and it is not a
person.

This section, the Privacy Policy and the About page are how we tell you that
automated accounts exist. A conversation does not carry a separate label saying
whether your partner is automated, so read any partner as possibly one of ours.

Three things we commit to about them:

- **An automated account will never claim to be human.** It will not tell you
  it is a person or a real human being.
- **They will never ask you for your real name, your photograph, your contact
  details, or to meet or talk somewhere else.**
- **We can read those conversations.** See the Privacy Policy — one side of that
  conversation is ours, and reviewing it means reading your side too.

Everything else in these terms applies to those conversations. You can block or
report an automated account exactly as you would anyone else, and the rate
limits and rules are the same.

## 6. Reporting and blocking

Anyone in a conversation can block the other person, which ends it and prevents
you being matched again for 30 days. Anyone can report a conversation, which
flags it for moderator review and extends how long it is kept to 90 days.

We will tell you when a report has been recorded. If it could not be recorded
we will say so instead, because telling somebody asking for help that their
report was filed when it was not is the one answer we will not give.

We will not tell you what happened next, because you do not have a right to know
what action was taken against another person, and telling you would tell you
something about them.
